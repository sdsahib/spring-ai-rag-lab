# Cancelling a Subscription

An AcmeFlow Pro subscription can be cancelled only by the workspace owner from **Settings > Billing > Manage subscription**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current monthly or annual billing period. The workspace keeps Pro features until that date, and cancellation does not produce a prorated refund.

At the end of the paid period, the workspace moves to the Free plan. Projects and attachments are not immediately removed. If the workspace has more than five members, ten active projects, or 1 GB of files, existing content remains readable but new projects, invitations, and uploads are blocked until usage is reduced or Pro is restored.

Cancelling a subscription is different from deleting a workspace. Cancellation preserves data under the normal retention policy. Workspace deletion permanently schedules all workspace content for removal and should be used only when the team no longer needs its data.

Owners can reverse a pending cancellation before the renewal date from the same billing page. After the downgrade occurs, Pro can be purchased again at the current price; previous promotional pricing is not guaranteed.
