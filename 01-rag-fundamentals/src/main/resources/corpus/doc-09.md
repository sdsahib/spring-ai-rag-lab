# Getting Support

All AcmeFlow users can search the help center and contact support by emailing `support@acmeflow.example`. Include the workspace name, a short description, steps to reproduce the problem, and any visible error message. Do not send passwords, API keys, reset codes, or complete payment-card numbers.

Free plan requests receive a response on a best-effort basis, typically within three business days. Pro plan customers receive priority email support with a target first response within one business day. These are response targets, not guaranteed resolution times. Support hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00-17:00 UTC, excluding published holidays.

For widespread availability problems, check `status.acmeflow.example` before opening a request. The status page reports active incidents, planned maintenance, and service recovery updates. Sending multiple requests about the same incident does not accelerate a response.

Billing questions should include the invoice number but no card details. Privacy requests, including account-level data copies and deletion questions, should be sent to `privacy@acmeflow.example` so they reach the appropriate team.
