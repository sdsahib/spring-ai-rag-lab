# Creating an AcmeFlow Account

AcmeFlow is a fictional project-management service used in this RAG demo. To create an account, visit the sign-up page and enter your name, work email address, and a password of at least 12 characters. You must accept the terms of service before continuing.

AcmeFlow sends a verification link to the supplied email address. The link expires after 24 hours. If it expires, return to the sign-in page and select **Resend verification email**. An account cannot create projects or invite teammates until its email address is verified.

New accounts begin on the Free plan. A workspace is created automatically, and the person who registers becomes its owner. The owner can rename the workspace and invite up to four additional members. Each email address can belong to multiple workspaces, so a separate account is not required for every team.

Single sign-on with Google or Microsoft is also available. Social sign-in users do not create an AcmeFlow password, but they must use the same email address whenever they sign in.
