# Pro Plan

The AcmeFlow Pro plan is designed for growing teams that need higher limits and administrative controls. It costs $12 per active member per month when billed monthly, or $120 per active member per year when billed annually. Workspace owners can start a 14-day trial without entering payment details.

Pro includes unlimited projects, 100 GB of workspace storage, custom roles, guest access, audit-log export, and priority email support. It also raises the shared API allowance to 5,000 requests per hour. Single sign-on using SAML is available only on Pro workspaces with at least ten paid members.

Billing is based on active members, including the workspace owner. Guests are free and can access only projects to which they are invited. Members deactivated before the next renewal are excluded from the upcoming invoice; partial-month refunds are not issued.

If a Pro subscription expires or is downgraded, the workspace moves to Free plan limits. Data is not immediately deleted. Existing content remains readable, while creation and uploads are restricted until the workspace is within the Free plan allowances.
