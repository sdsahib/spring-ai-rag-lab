# Billing and Invoices

Only an AcmeFlow workspace owner or billing administrator can change payment details, view invoices, or switch billing frequency. These controls are available under **Settings > Billing**. AcmeFlow accepts major credit cards; bank transfer is available for annual subscriptions with at least 50 members.

Monthly subscriptions renew on the same calendar day each month. Annual subscriptions renew on the purchase anniversary. When members are added during a billing period, AcmeFlow applies a prorated charge for the remaining time. Removing or deactivating members changes the seat count at the next renewal and does not generate a partial refund.

Invoices are generated after every successful charge and can be downloaded as PDF files. Company name, billing address, and tax identification number should be updated before renewal because finalized invoices cannot be edited.

If a payment fails, AcmeFlow retries it over a seven-day grace period and emails the owner. The workspace remains usable during that period. After the final failed attempt, the subscription is downgraded to Free; content remains readable, subject to Free plan creation and storage restrictions.
