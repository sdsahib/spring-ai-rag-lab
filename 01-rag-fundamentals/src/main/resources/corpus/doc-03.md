# Free Plan

The AcmeFlow Free plan is intended for individuals and small teams evaluating the service. It costs nothing and supports one workspace with up to five members. A workspace can contain ten active projects and 1 GB of file storage. Archived projects do not count toward the project limit, but their attachments still use storage.

Free workspaces include task boards, comments, due dates, email notifications, and basic search. They do not include custom roles, audit-log export, single sign-on, or priority support. API access is available with a limit of 100 requests per hour for the entire workspace.

There is no trial deadline and no payment method is required. If the workspace exceeds a limit after downgrading from Pro, existing data remains readable, but members cannot create new projects or upload files until usage falls within Free plan limits.

Workspace owners can upgrade at any time from **Settings > Billing**. Upgrading keeps all projects, members, and attachments in place. Free workspaces that remain completely inactive for 12 months may be scheduled for deletion after email notice to the owner.
