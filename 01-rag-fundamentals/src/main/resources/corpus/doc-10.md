# Data Retention and Deletion

AcmeFlow retains active workspace content for as long as the workspace exists. Cancelling Pro does not delete data; it moves the workspace to the Free plan at the end of the billing period. Free workspaces that have no sign-ins or activity for 12 consecutive months may be scheduled for deletion after 30 days' email notice to the owner.

When an owner deletes a workspace, it enters a 30-day recovery period. During this period the workspace is inaccessible, but support can restore it after verifying the owner's identity. After 30 days, projects, tasks, comments, and attachments are permanently removed from active systems. Encrypted backup copies may persist for up to 90 additional days before automatic expiration.

Audit events for Pro workspaces are available to administrators for 365 days. Billing invoices are retained for seven years where required for tax and accounting obligations, even if the related workspace is deleted.

Members can ask the owner to remove their profile from a workspace. Requests involving personal-data access or deletion can also be sent to `privacy@acmeflow.example`. Before deleting a workspace, owners should create a data export because deletion cannot be reversed after the recovery period.
